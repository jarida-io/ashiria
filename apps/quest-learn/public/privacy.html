Ashiria Learn ("the app") is a Kenyan Sign Language practice app for Meta Quest, made by Jarida ("we") in Nairobi, Kenya. It is a web app that runs in the Meta Quest Browser. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. In short: your hand movements are processed on your headset and never leave it, the app has no account, and it collects no analytics.

What the app uses, and why

InformationWhat the app does with itWhere it stays
Hand tracking (positions of your hand and finger joints, provided by the headset)Compares your signing with the reference signs to give feedback, for example "bend your ring finger"Processed in real time on your headset. Never stored as a recording, never sent anywhere
Head position and direction (from the headset)Works out where your hands are relative to you, and places menusProcessed in real time on your headset
Your progress (signs practised and learned, streaks, scores)Shows your progress and picks what to practise nextSaved in the browser's storage on your headset only
Your settings (signing hand, strictness, rest height, accessibility options)Adapts the app to you, for example how strictly signs are checkedSaved in the browser's storage on your headset only
Passthrough (camera view of your room), if you use mixed-reality featuresLets you see your room while practising. The app never receives or stores camera images; the headset composes the viewNot accessible to the app

The app does not:

  • record or upload your hand movements;
  • use your hand data for anything except making hand tracking work in the app;
  • collect analytics, advertising identifiers, contacts, location or voice;
  • show adverts;
  • have an account, sign-in or social features.

Loading the app. Like any web page, the app's files are downloaded from our web host ([host, e.g. Vercel]) when you open the link. The host may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address and browser type, for a short time to run and protect the service. Apart from loading its own files, the app makes no network requests. Your hand data, progress and settings are never sent.

Deleting your data

Press Delete my data on the lesson panel, or Start over on the start page, to erase your progress and settings from the headset immediately. Clearing this site's data in the Meta Quest Browser also deletes everything the app saved.

Children

The app is rated for teens and adults (13+). It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Sign content

The reference signs in the app are hand movements derived, with permission, from the KSL Dictionary's sign videos, plus some recorded by the developer. The app contains only that hand-movement data: no videos or images of anyone.

Meta

The app runs in the Meta Quest Browser on Meta Quest. Hand tracking, passthrough and the browser itself are provided by Meta, and Meta's handling of data from your headset and account is covered by Meta's own privacy policy, not this one.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data: [contact email], Jarida, Nairobi, Kenya. Because the app stores nothing off your headset, we hold no personal data about you. You stay in control of everything on the device.

Changes

We will update this page, and the date at the top, before any change to how the app handles information.
